Under the hood

This page takes software apart for a living — so it documents its own insides. The whole thing ships as one folder with no framework and no server.

src/*.ts13 strict TypeScript modules
→
esbuildone bundle, ~30 kB
→
scripts.jsthe page you are reading
  • 16 scenes — 4 seasons × 4 times of day, picked by the real clock in Astana (UTC+5). Only one video loads first.
  • No framework — the DOM is the state. Everything works offline; the single-file build embeds video, audio and fonts as data URLs.
  • Accessibility first — keyboard, screen readers and reduced-motion are tested at 4 viewport sizes in Playwright.
  • Mika — the rooftop mascot is positioned by mirroring the video’s cover-crop math, so she is clickable at every aspect ratio.
