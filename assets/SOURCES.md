# Visual asset sources

All 16 background videos were regenerated on 2026-10-12 with Gemini Omni 1.1 Flash using the corresponding JPG as both first and last frame. `spring_city_hero.mp4` was generated at 1080p in Google AI Studio; the other 15 were generated at 720p in Google Flow and exported using its built-in 1080p upscale. Final files are 1920×1080, 24 fps, approximately 10 seconds. The background copies preserve the generated video streams, remove audio, and use MP4 fast start. Outputs were matched to the original images, visually reviewed across the clip and loop boundary, and fully decoded to check for errors. The winter daytime and autumn nighttime scenes were regenerated again to correct mismatched seasonal effects.

UI icons: [Lucide](https://lucide.dev/), downloaded from the [official SVG source](https://github.com/lucide-icons/lucide/tree/main/icons). License: [ISC notice](licenses/lucide.txt). Selected icons are inlined as SVG symbols in index.html and work offline. The `assets/icons/` folder keeps the source SVGs.

Project visuals are original SVG schematics drawn for this site (inline in index.html). They illustrate each tool's purpose and are not screenshots of the programs. No stock photography is used.

Typography: [Onest](https://github.com/simpals/onest), the latin variable subset from the [@fontsource-variable/onest](https://www.npmjs.com/package/@fontsource-variable/onest) package (fontsource.org). SIL OFL 1.1 notice: licenses/onest.txt. The woff2 is bundled locally and embedded in the standalone build.

GitHub brand mark: [Simple Icons](https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons), CC0 notice in licenses/simple-icons.txt.
